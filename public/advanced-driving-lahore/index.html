Who This Is For

Some drivers can move a car but freeze at a busy roundabout. Some passed their test years ago but have barely driven since. Others can handle quiet streets but panic when Lahore traffic gets heavy. If any of that sounds familiar, you do not need to start from scratch. You need targeted practice on the exact situations that make you nervous.

The advanced training at Razia Driving Center is mapped to the PRO+ plan: 15 days of one-to-one lessons focused on road confidence rather than basic control. It is the right fit once you understand the clutch and gears but still do not feel safe driving alone.

What Advanced Training Covers

Heavy Traffic

Real practice on crowded Lahore roads, learning to read other drivers, keep safe gaps and stay calm in stop-and-go flow.

Complex Parking

Parallel parking on busy streets, tight bay parking, and reversing into narrow spots with traffic behind you.

Roundabouts & Junctions

Multi-lane roundabouts, unmarked junctions, and the small judgement calls that prevent hesitation and accidents.

Night Driving

Evening lessons that teach you to use headlights correctly, manage glare and judge distance after dark.

Lane Discipline

Staying in lane at speed, safe merging, overtaking only when appropriate, and using mirrors as a habit.

Road Judgement

Predicting what pedestrians, rickshaws and other cars might do before they do it. This is what makes a driver safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

I have a licence but I rarely drive. Is this for me?

Yes. Many students join after holding a licence for months or years without feeling confident. The PRO+ plan builds the real-world practice that the test alone does not give you.

Do I need to know the basics already?

Yes. This training assumes you can control the car. If you are starting from zero, the Basic or PLUS plan is the right place to begin.

Will you teach me on the motorway?

The focus is city traffic, which is where most drivers lack confidence. Motorway practice can be discussed with the instructor, but it is not the core of the plan.

Can I take evening lessons?

Yes. Lessons run until 8 PM, and the advanced plan includes night driving practice so you are comfortable after dark.

What if 15 days is not enough?

You can book extra lessons after the plan ends. The instructor will tell you honestly when you are ready to drive alone.
