Before You Touch the Car

Before your first lesson on a public road, you need a valid CNIC and a learner licence from the driving licence office in your tehsil. The learner requires a small fee, a medical form, and a basic traffic-rules test. Apply first and use the waiting time to read the basic road signs.

What Happens on Day 1

Your first lesson is not about driving fast. It is about building trust between your feet, your hands and the car. Here is what a normal first session looks like with Razia Driving Center:

1 Seat & mirrors. The instructor adjusts your seat and mirrors so you can press the clutch fully and see clearly without twisting your body.

2 Controls explained. You learn what the clutch, brake, accelerator, gear lever and handbrake do, and how they work together.

3 Finding the bite. You practice finding the clutch bite point again and again until it becomes a habit.

4 First moves. You shift into first gear, find the bite, and roll forward slowly. Then you stop smoothly.

Stalling on day 1 is normal. The instructor's car has dual controls, so you are always safe.

Clutch Bite: The Single Most Important Skill

In a manual car, the clutch connects the engine to the wheels. The bite point is the exact moment that connection starts. To find it: press the clutch fully, shift to first gear, and release the clutch very slowly. When the engine sound drops slightly and the car wants to move, hold that point for a second before releasing further. That is how you move without stalling.

Tip: Practice the bite on a flat road first. Master flat ground before attempting hill starts.

Lane Position, Roundabouts and Night Driving

Pick a reference point on the bonnet to judge your car width, and keep a door-width gap from parked cars. At roundabouts, slow down, look right, give way to traffic already on the circle, and keep moving once you have a safe gap. Hesitation is more dangerous than careful entry.

After dark, visibility drops and glare is distracting. Use low beams when there is traffic ahead, high beams only on empty roads, and never stare at oncoming lights. Take at least one lesson with an instructor at night before driving alone after dark.

When to Book Formal Lessons

Book lessons when you stall often, cannot park cleanly, freeze in traffic, or simply want to learn faster than self-practice allows. A few one-to-one lessons with an instructor will fix most gaps in days rather than months.

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need before lessons?

A valid CNIC and a learner licence. The learner licence is required by law before you drive on public roads.

What happens on day 1?

Seat and mirror adjustment, controls explanation, finding the clutch bite, and first slow moves in first gear.

How do I find the clutch bite point?

Release the clutch slowly in first gear. When the engine sound drops slightly and the car wants to move, that is the bite point. Hold it for a second before releasing further.

When should I book lessons?

As soon as you have your learner licence, or whenever you feel stuck with stalling, parking or traffic nerves.

How do I handle roundabouts?

Slow down, look right, give way to traffic already on the roundabout, signal your exit and keep moving once there is a safe gap.

Is night driving different?

Yes. Visibility is lower and glare is distracting. Practice with an instructor after dark before driving alone at night.
