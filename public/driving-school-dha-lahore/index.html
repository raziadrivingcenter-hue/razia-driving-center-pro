Driving Lessons Across DHA Lahore

Razia Driving Center provides driving lessons to students across all phases of DHA Lahore. Whether you live in DHA Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, Phase 4, Phase 5, Phase 6, Phase 7, Phase 8, or the Y and Z Blocks, the instructor comes to your area for training.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cover all DHA phases?

Yes. We provide driving lessons across DHA Phase 1 through Phase 8, Y Block, Z Block, and DHA Defence Road areas.

Is pick and drop available in DHA?

Yes. The instructor picks you from your home in DHA, teaches on roads in your own area, then drops you back home after the lesson.

Which car is used for training?

Training is on a manual car with dual controls. The instructor has full control of the brake and clutch from the passenger seat for safety.

Can complete beginners join from DHA?

Yes. The Basic Plan of 7 days is designed for complete beginners. No prior driving experience is needed.

What are the course fees?

The Basic Plan is Rs. 9,999, the PLUS Plan is Rs. 14,500, and the PRO+ Plan is Rs. 21,750. A pick and drop charge applies based on your distance from the center.
