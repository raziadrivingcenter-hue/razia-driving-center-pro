What Is a Learner Driving License?

A learner driving license is a temporary permit issued by the Punjab government that allows you to learn driving on public roads under supervision. It is not a full driving license. With a learner permit, you must:

  • Display L-plates on the front and rear of the vehicle
  • Be accompanied by a person who holds a valid permanent driving license
  • Follow all traffic rules and restrictions that apply to learner drivers

Once you have your learner permit and have practiced enough, you can apply for a permanent driving license by passing the practical driving test.

Documents Required for an Online Learner License

Before you start the application, have these ready as digital files (scanned or photographed):

  • CNIC Front & Back — Clear scanned copies or photos of both sides of your CNIC
  • Passport-size photograph — Recent, white or light background, clearly showing your face
  • CNIC Number — 13-digit format (XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X)
  • Date of Birth — As per your CNIC
  • Mobile Number — For account registration and SMS updates
  • Email Address — For your Dastak account

For doorstep service only: Keep your original CNIC and documents at home. The facilitator will collect them when they visit.

How to Apply for a Learner License Online Through Dastak

The Dastak portal offers two ways to apply: Self-Service (you complete everything online, then collect documents from the office) and Doorstep Service (a facilitator comes to your home). Here is the step-by-step process.

Step 1 — Open the Official Dastak Website

Go to dastak.punjab.gov.pk/citizen/services in your browser. The portal works on both desktop and mobile.

Step 2 — Register or Sign In

If you do not have a Dastak account, click Create Account and fill in:

  • Mobile number (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone, or SCOM)
  • CNIC number (13 digits)
  • Email address
  • Password and confirm password
  • CAPTCHA verification

After registration, you can optionally Connect DLIMS Account (to bring existing applications into Dastak) or Skip to proceed to login.

Step 3 — Select Learner Driving License

From the services page, find DLIMS Services and select Learner Driving License. The portal will show you two service options.

Step 4 — Choose Self-Service or Doorstep Service

Self-ServiceYou complete the application online. Collect your final documents from the relevant office in person.
Doorstep ServiceA facilitator collects documents and fee from your home. No office visit required. Extra facilitator fee applies.

Important: Switching between self-service and doorstep service removes all entered information. Choose carefully before filling the form.

Step 5 — Enter Applicant Details

Fill in your Citizen Profile with:

  • Name, Father Name / Husband Name
  • Phone number, Emergency contact number
  • Gender, Height
  • Citizen Type, Vehicle Type, Nationality
  • Blood Group, Disability status
  • Permanent Address (Province, District, City) and Current Address

Step 6 — Upload the Required Documents

Upload clear digital copies of:

  • Your passport-size photograph
  • CNIC Front side
  • CNIC Back side

Make sure the images are clear and all text is readable. Blurry uploads are a common reason for delays.

Step 7 — Review and Pay the Fee

Review all entered information carefully. Certify that the information is correct (you are liable for legal action if found false). Then pay the displayed fee through the portal.

Step 8 — Submit and Track Your Application

After successful submission, you will see a confirmation message. You can track your application status using your CNIC number on the Dastak portal.

How Long Does It Take to Receive a Learner License?

The official portal lists the turnaround time as Working Days for self-service and 04 Working Days for doorstep service. Actual processing may vary depending on application volume and verification. Track your status on the portal using your CNIC.

How Long Is a Learner Permit Valid?

A learner driving permit in Punjab is valid for 180 days (6 months) from the date of issue. Use this time to take proper driving lessons and prepare for your permanent license test. If your learner expires before you get your permanent license, you may need to reapply.

Common Problems During the Application

Account Registration Issues

If the CAPTCHA does not load or registration fails, try refreshing the page or using a different browser. Make sure your CNIC is entered in the correct 13-digit format (XXXXX-XXXXXXX-X).

Photo Upload Rejected

Use a clear, recent passport-size photo with a plain background. Avoid selfies, group photos, or images with shadows on the face. The file should be in a standard format (JPG/PNG) and within the size limit shown on the portal.

CNIC Scans Not Clear

Scan or photograph your CNIC in good lighting. All four corners should be visible and all text readable. Do not use filters or editing apps.

Payment Failed

If the payment does not go through, check your mobile account balance or card limit. Try again after a few minutes. If the problem persists, contact Dastak support at support@dastak.punjab.gov.pk or call 1202.

Application Status Not Updating

Processing takes time. If your status has not changed after the expected working days, contact Dastak support with your CNIC and application details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for a learner license online in Punjab?

Yes. Apply through the official Dastak portal at dastak.punjab.gov.pk under DLIMS Services.

Which website should I use?

Use the official Dastak Punjab portal: https://dastak.punjab.gov.pk/citizen/services. Select Learner Driving License under DLIMS Services.

What documents are required?

You need a valid CNIC, a recent passport-size photograph (digital), and scanned copies of your CNIC front and back. For doorstep service, keep original documents ready for the facilitator.

How much does a learner license cost?

Self-service: PKR 950 + PKR 19 platform fee. Doorstep: PKR 1,000 + PKR 1,000 facilitator + PKR 1,000 platform. Confirm the final amount on the portal before paying.

Can I apply using my mobile phone?

Yes. The Dastak portal works on mobile browsers. You can complete the entire application including document uploads from your phone.

How can I track my application?

Track your status using your CNIC number on the Dastak portal after submission.

How long is the learner permit valid?

A learner driving permit in Punjab is valid for 180 days (6 months) from the date of issue.

Is a learner permit the same as a permanent license?

No. A learner permit allows you to learn driving under supervision. A permanent license is issued after you pass the practical driving test.

Can I choose doorstep service?

Yes. A facilitator collects documents and fee from your home. No office visit required. An additional facilitator fee applies.

Where can I get help if the portal does not work?

Contact Dastak support at support@dastak.punjab.gov.pk or call 1202. For driving lessons, WhatsApp Razia Driving Center at 0309-4461407.
