Pick & Drop Charges

Pick and drop is available as an additional service. The formula is:

Pick & Drop Fee = Distance (km) × 50 × Course Days × 2

For example, if you live 5 km away and take the 10-day PLUS Plan:

5 km × 50 × 10 days × 2 = Rs. 5,000

Note: The first 2 km are free only on the PLUS Plan. The maximum distance for pick and drop is 20 km.
