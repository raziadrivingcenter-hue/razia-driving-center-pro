Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in each driving course?

Each course includes one-to-one practical lessons of about 30 minutes each on a manual car with dual controls. The Basic Plan has 7 days, the PLUS Plan has 10 days, and the PRO+ Plan has 15 days. Training covers starting, stopping, clutch and gears, steering, parking, reversing and real traffic.

How does pick and drop work?

The instructor picks the student up from home, teaches on roads in the student's own area, then drops them home. Training is not at the center.

How is the pick and drop charge calculated?

The formula is distance in km multiplied by 50, multiplied by the course days, multiplied by 2. On the PLUS Plan only, the first 2 km are free. On the Basic and PRO+ plans, the full distance is charged. The maximum covered distance is 20 km.

Which plan do most learners choose?

The PLUS Plan of 10 days is the most popular. It covers normal Lahore traffic, parking and reversing, and the first 2 km of pick and drop are free on this plan.

Will I get a license after the course?

The course builds your driving skill and confidence. It does not promise a license. The level you reach depends on the plan and your practice.
