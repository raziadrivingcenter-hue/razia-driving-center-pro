Who These Lessons Are For

These lessons are for anyone in Lahore who wants to learn car driving from scratch or fix the gaps in their basic training. If you have never sat in the driver seat, if you know the theory but freeze in traffic, or if you can move the car but cannot park with confidence, the training is built around those exact problems.

Lessons are one-to-one and taught by Madam Razia, a female instructor. Many students, especially women and younger drivers, prefer this setup because it removes the stress of a crowded or mixed-giving environment.

What You Learn, Lesson by Lesson

Lesson 1 — Cockpit & Controls

How to adjust the seat, set mirrors, understand the clutch, brake, accelerator, gear lever and steering. First smooth starts and stops.

Lesson 2 — Clutch Bite & Low Speed

Finding the bite point, moving without stalling, gear 1 to 2 changes, and keeping a steady speed on a quiet stretch.

Lesson 3 — Steering & Lane Position

Holding the road correctly, turning at T-junctions, and judging the width of your own car.

Lesson 4 — Parking & Reversing

Parallel parking, bay parking, and reversing on a narrow street with the instructor guiding every move.

Lesson 5 — Real Traffic

Joining main roads, reading other drivers, handling roundabouts and crossing busy Lahore intersections.

Lesson 6 & Beyond — Confidence

Heavy traffic, night driving practice if needed, highway merging, and the small decisions that separate a nervous driver from a safe one.

Frequently Asked Questions

I have never driven before. Can I really learn in 7 days?

Many students do. Seven days gives you the core skills: smooth control, parking, and light traffic. If you need more time, you can extend to the 10 or 15 day plan.

Do you teach in a manual or automatic car?

Training is in a manual car. Learning manual gives you the skill to drive any car, and the instructor has full control with dual pedals.

What if I stall the car during a lesson?

Stalling is part of learning. The car has dual controls, so the instructor keeps you safe and helps you recover every time.

Can I choose my lesson time?

Yes. Lessons run from 8 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Sunday. Book a slot that fits your routine.

Is parking part of the training?

Yes. Parallel parking, bay parking and reversing are covered in every plan.
